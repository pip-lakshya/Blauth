# BLAuth

### Your Identity. Your Credentials. Your Control.

BLAuth is a **privacy-first, blockchain-backed biometric authentication system** that lets users prove their identity to different applications without exposing more personal information than necessary.

Instead of every website storing its own identity data, BLAuth acts as an authentication layer between the user and the application.

A user enrolls once, verifies locally using their biometric, and receives a blockchain-backed identity proof. Developers can integrate BLAuth into their applications and request only the identity attributes they actually need.

---

## 🚀 The Problem

Traditional authentication has two major problems:

1. **Identity data is centralized.**
   Websites and platforms store sensitive personal information such as names, student IDs, phone numbers and dates of birth.

2. **Authentication and data sharing are coupled.**
   A website often receives more information than it actually needs.
